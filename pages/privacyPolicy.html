Denmaq Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Denmaq," "we," "us," or "our") is a full-service technology engineering and digital transformation agency. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through our website denmaq.com, client service engagements, project communications, and talent recruitment processes, how we collect and process it, and your legal rights regarding your personal data.

1Introduction

Denmaq Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India (LG-05, H-202, Dallas 1 Business Park, Sector-63, Noida, UP - 201309). We specialize in custom software development, AI solutions, mobile application engineering, cloud infrastructure, website design, and enterprise digital marketing.

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors to our website, prospective and existing clients, partners, job applicants, and individuals who communicate with us. By visiting denmaq.com or submitting information through our portal, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide efficient, secure, and tailored technology services. The types of data we gather include:

Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact & Inquiries: Your full name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and project requirement details shared via our contact or consultation forms.
  • Service & Project Engagements: Business documentation, billing information, repository access credentials, project specifications, and NDA records shared during client onboarding.
  • Career Applications: Resumes/CVs, cover letters, portfolio links, employment history, and contact details submitted through our career portal.
  • Marketing Subscriptions: Email addresses and preferences provided when subscribing to company newsletters or technical insights.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referral source, and time zone settings.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, duration of visit, links clicked, scroll depth, and interaction metrics gathered through server logs and analytics cookies.

3How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Delivering, operating, maintaining, and scaling custom software and digital agency services.
  • Evaluating project proposals, scoping deliverables, and executing client agreements.
  • Processing job applications and conducting talent recruitment interviews.
  • Communicating project updates, administrative notices, and technical security alerts.
  • Analyzing website performance, maintaining cyber security, and preventing fraudulent activities.
  • Complying with applicable Indian and legal tax laws, corporate records, and regulatory mandates.

4Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and local browser storage to provide a seamless browsing experience, remember your visual theme preference (light/dark theme), and analyze website usage.

Cookie Type Function & Purpose
Essential Cookies Necessary for core website navigation, secure form submission, and server session management.
Preference Cookies Remembers your site preferences, including theme toggle states and UI settings.
Analytics Cookies Aggregates anonymous usage statistics to help us optimize user experience and page performance (e.g., Google Analytics).

You can adjust your cookie settings through your web browser preferences. Disabling certain cookies may impact site functionality.

5How We Share Your Information

Denmaq does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We share data only with:

  • Service Infrastructure Vendors: Trusted cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS, Cloudflare), analytics partners, and communication infrastructure tools bound by strict non-disclosure agreements.
  • Legal Compliance & Protection: Authorized judicial or government bodies when mandatory under applicable laws, court summons, or regulatory enforcement.
  • Business Reorganization: Successor entities in the event of a merger, acquisition, joint venture, or asset consolidation, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

6Data Security

Denmaq implements enterprise-grade technical, physical, and organizational security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, data leaks, or alteration of personal information. Our safety measures include 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit, firewalls, role-based database access, and regular vulnerability audits.

While we maintain rigorous security protocols, no internet transmission or digital storage method is 100% immune to risk. We recommend using secure networks and protecting sensitive credentials.

7Data Retention

We retain personal information only for the duration required to satisfy the business purposes outlined in this policy, or as mandated by statutory compliance, tax reporting, and dispute resolution guidelines. Candidate resumes are retained for active recruitment cycles before secure deletion or anonymization.

8Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable data protection frameworks (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and international privacy laws like GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access & Inspection: Request a copy of the personal information Denmaq maintains about you.
  • Correction & Updates: Request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal data.
  • Erasure & Deletion: Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to ongoing contract and legal retention mandates.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link or contacting us directly.

9Children's Privacy

Denmaq's website and software services are designed exclusively for businesses and working professionals over the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under 18 years of age.

10International Data Transfers

If you are visiting our website from outside India, please note that your information may be processed and stored in servers located within India or hosted on cloud infrastructure globally. We take appropriate contractual and technical steps to ensure your data receives robust protection across borders.

11Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external partner platforms, client case studies, and social media channels (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, GitHub). This Privacy Policy applies strictly to denmaq.com. We encourage you to inspect the privacy notices of any external websites you visit.

12Changes to This Policy

Denmaq Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated modification timestamp. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.
