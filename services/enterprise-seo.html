Service Overview

What is Enterprise SEO?

Enterprise SEO is a highly strategic approach to search engine optimization designed specifically for massive websites—typically those with tens of thousands to millions of pages. This includes massive e-commerce sites, international publications, aggregators, and Fortune 500 corporate domains.

At the enterprise level, the challenges change. It is no longer just about writing a few blog posts. It is about managing crawl budgets, preventing massive index bloat, automating technical fixes at scale, aligning with complex corporate IT structures, and protecting established organic revenue streams from algorithmic turbulence. Denmaq provides the sophistication and resources required to maneuver large organizations through the complexities of modern search.

Success Story

Case Study: Global Job Aggregator

Managing Crawl Budget for 5 Million URLs

Problem: A major job board with 5 million dynamically generated URLs was suffering from severe index bloat. Google was crawling expired job listings and ignoring new ones.
Solution: We implemented a strict crawl budget strategy, utilizing dynamic robots.txt rules, robust pagination canonicals, and automated expiration status codes (410) for old jobs.
Result: Crawl efficiency improved by 400%. Google began indexing new jobs within minutes instead of days, leading to a 35% increase in organic traffic and application submissions.
