Choosing a Framework

Flutter vs React Native

Both are excellent choices - we help you pick the one that fits your product, team and timeline.

Flutter

  • Beautiful, highly customizable UI
  • Fast, compiled performance
  • Backed by Google
Best for: brand-heavy, animation-rich apps

React Native

  • Strong native feel and feedback
  • Massive JavaScript ecosystem
  • Backed by Meta
Best for: teams with existing JS/web expertise
