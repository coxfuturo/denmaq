    08

    Quality Assurance

    Executing automated unit tests, XCTest UI tests, security audits, and XCTest performance benchmarks.

    09

    App Store Deployment

    Distributing TestFlight beta builds, configuring App Store Connect metadata, and managing Apple publishing.

    10

    Maintenance & Updates

    Providing post-launch SLA support, iOS version upgrades, bug fixes, and feature enhancements.
