Best Commercial Refrigeration Companies In Vancouver

How to choose a refrigeration partner that can actually respond when a walk-in cooler goes down at 6am — not just the first name that comes up in a search.

By Denmaq August 20, 2026 9 min read

Vancouver's food service, grocery, and hospitality scene runs on equipment most customers never think about — walk-in coolers, reach-in freezers, ice machines, and produce cases that have to hold temperature every hour of every day. When that equipment fails, it isn't a minor inconvenience; it's spoiled inventory, a possible health code issue, and a business that can't operate until someone shows up with the right parts.

The Lower Mainland has a mix of large HVAC-and-refrigeration firms that have served commercial kitchens for decades and smaller specialist shops built around fast emergency response. A restaurant in Gastown, a grocery store in Kitsilano, and a food distribution warehouse in Burnaby don't need the exact same refrigeration partner, even though the marketing on most company websites reads the same regardless of who's asking. That's why choosing a commercial refrigeration company isn't just about finding someone who answers the phone — it's about finding one whose response times, service area, and design/build experience actually match your operation. Here's what to look for, and which local names hold up.

Key Evaluation Criteria

✓

True 24/7 emergency response

Ask what "24/7" actually means — a live technician on call, or a voicemail that gets returned the next business day. A refrigeration failure doesn't wait for office hours.

✓

Experience with your equipment brands

Confirm the technician who shows up actually services the make and model in your kitchen, not just a generalist who has to order parts before starting the repair.

✓

Design/build capability, if you need it

A new walk-in cooler or a kitchen buildout needs a company that can design and install a system, not just repair one that already exists.

✓

A real preventative maintenance plan

Scheduled maintenance catches a failing compressor before it fails during service. Ask what a maintenance visit actually includes, not just how often it happens.

✓

Clear service area and response time

A supplier based in Burnaby or the Fraser Valley may quote a longer response time to a downtown Vancouver kitchen — confirm coverage before an emergency, not during one.

Pro tip: Ask a prospective refrigeration company for their average emergency response time in writing, and get it in the service agreement. A verbal promise during the sales call isn't the same as a contractual commitment.

04

Red Flags to Avoid

✕

No firm emergency response commitment

If a company can't give you a specific response window in writing, "24/7 service" may just be marketing language.

✕

Unclear or missing service area

Some companies advertise across the whole Lower Mainland but subcontract service outside their home base — ask directly who will actually show up at your door.

✕

No preventative maintenance option

A company that only does emergency repair, with no scheduled maintenance plan, is setting you up for repeat breakdowns instead of preventing them.

✕

Vague or bundled pricing

Ask for a breakdown of labor, parts, and any after-hours callout fee before the technician arrives, not after the invoice is written.

✕

No references from similar businesses

A company whose case studies are all large industrial clients may not prioritize a single-location restaurant the same way — ask for a comparable reference.

05

The Bottom Line

Vancouver has a deep bench of commercial refrigeration companies, from design/build veterans like M&T Air Conditioning and Fraser Valley Refrigeration down to fast-response specialists like Mr. Fridge and Greenway Mechanical. The right choice depends less on company size and more on whether a supplier's response time, service area, and equipment experience actually fit your operation.

A good refrigeration partner should be someone you never have to think about twice — because your equipment stays running, and when it doesn't, someone reliable is already on the way. Choose accordingly.
